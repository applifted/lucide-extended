Export every Lucide alias with the `Filled` and `Duotone` suffixes, as in `lucide-react`. For example, `AlertCircleFilled` is the same component as `CircleAlertFilled`, and renders its class name, `applifted-icon-circle-alert-filled`. Aliases are marked `@deprecated` and also work as deep imports, such as `lucide-extended-react/alert-circle-filled`.
